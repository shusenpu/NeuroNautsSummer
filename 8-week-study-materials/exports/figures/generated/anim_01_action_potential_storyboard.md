# anim_01_action_potential_storyboard: Action Potential Storyboard

**Placement:** Week D / Chapter 4. How Spikes Begin and Travel

**Caption:** Storyboard for an action potential animation. Each frame should pair channel state with the matching segment of the voltage trace.

**Alt text:** Text storyboard describing successive phases of an action potential animation.

**Source anchor:** Action potential and channel timing concepts from introductory neuroscience and OpenStax-style action-potential explanations.

## Frame 1. Resting State

- **Voltage trace:** V_m sits near -70 mV.
- **Na+ channels:** Closed and ready to open.
- **K+ channels:** Mostly closed, with leak pathways still present.
- **Narration:** The neuron is not silent chemically; it is maintaining ion gradients and a resting voltage.

## Frame 2. Graded Depolarization Approaches Threshold

- **Voltage trace:** V_m rises from about -70 mV toward -55 mV.
- **Na+ channels:** Some voltage-gated Na+ channels begin to open.
- **K+ channels:** Delayed voltage-gated K+ channels have not yet caught up.
- **Narration:** Synaptic input is still graded until the trigger zone crosses threshold.

## Frame 3. Threshold and Positive Feedback

- **Voltage trace:** V_m crosses threshold.
- **Na+ channels:** More Na+ channels open quickly.
- **K+ channels:** Still slower.
- **Narration:** Na+ entry makes the inside less negative, which opens more Na+ channels.

## Frame 4. Rising Phase

- **Voltage trace:** V_m rises steeply toward positive values.
- **Na+ channels:** Many are open.
- **K+ channels:** Beginning to open, but delayed.
- **Narration:** The action potential is now self-amplifying, which is why it is all-or-none.

## Frame 5. Peak and Na+ Inactivation

- **Voltage trace:** V_m peaks around +30 mV in the teaching trace.
- **Na+ channels:** Many inactivate and stop passing Na+.
- **K+ channels:** More are open.
- **Narration:** The spike does not keep rising because Na+ channels inactivate.

## Frame 6. Repolarization

- **Voltage trace:** V_m falls back down.
- **Na+ channels:** Inactivated or returning toward closed-ready states.
- **K+ channels:** Open and letting K+ current pull V_m downward.
- **Narration:** K+ effects help reset the membrane.

## Frame 7. After-Hyperpolarization

- **Voltage trace:** V_m briefly dips below resting level.
- **Na+ channels:** Recovering from inactivation.
- **K+ channels:** Some remain open longer than needed.
- **Narration:** The delayed K+ conductance can make the membrane more negative than rest for a short time.

## Frame 8. Refractory Recovery

- **Voltage trace:** V_m returns toward -70 mV.
- **Na+ channels:** Reset to closed-ready.
- **K+ channels:** Return toward resting state.
- **Narration:** The refractory period helps make spike travel one-way and limits how fast spikes can repeat.

## Student Check

Cover the frame titles and use only the channel states to reconstruct the voltage trace. Then cover the channel states and use only the voltage trace to explain the channel story.
