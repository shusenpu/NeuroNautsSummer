# Chapter 1 Outline: Neuron Shape, Signal Direction, and the Input-Decision-Output Map

## 1. Opening Question

- Question: How can one living cell receive many signals, make a decision, and send an output in one direction?
- Target answer: A neuron uses specialized regions: dendrites receive, soma/axon hillock integrate, axon carries, terminals communicate.
- Current source: `minibook_v2/source/chapters/neurons_as_signaling_cells.md`.

## 2. Why This Matters

- Use this chapter to build the basic map for every later mechanism.
- Connect shape to function before introducing ions and voltage.
- Keep tone concrete: neurons are cells, not wires, but they solve a wire-like communication problem.

## 3. Visual First

- Required visual: `fig_01_real_neuron_gallery.svg`.
- Companion visual: `fig_02_neuron_map.svg`.
- Student task: mark receiving region, decision region, output path, and communication end.

## 4. Core Concepts And Terms To Define

- Subsection headings: Real neurons are diverse; A neuron is still a cell; Input regions; Decision region; Output region; Direction of signal flow; First voltage words.
- Exact terms to define: neuron, dendrite, soma, axon hillock, axon, axon terminal, synapse, membrane, charge, voltage, membrane potential.
- Keep terms short and paired with a drawing.

## 5. Read The Graph

- Graph to read: simple membrane voltage change graph from -70 mV to -55 mV.
- Student reads: x-axis time, y-axis membrane potential in mV, upward change means less negative.
- Skill: distinguish sign from size of change.

## 6. Worked Example And Try One Yourself

- Worked example: A membrane changes from -70 mV to -55 mV. What happened and by how much?
- Expected solution: depolarized by 15 mV; became less negative.
- Try one: -70 mV to -80 mV; answer: hyperpolarized by 10 mV.

## 7. Mini Lab Or Simulation

- Mini-lab: neuron morphology tracing.
- Materials: `toy_pyramidal_morphology.swc` or printed neuron images.
- Student output: one labeled drawing plus a one-sentence input-decision-output explanation.

## 8. Assessment And Teacher Note

- 5-question quiz:
  1. Label dendrite, soma, axon, and terminal.
  2. Predict signal direction on a neuron drawing.
  3. Explain why real neurons do not all look alike.
  4. Calculate the change from -65 mV to -50 mV.
  5. State the difference between charge and voltage.
- Teacher note: likely misconceptions are that every neuron has the same cartoon shape, that axons and dendrites are interchangeable, and that voltage is a substance moving down the axon. Keep returning to regions and roles.
