# Alt Text Catalog

## `fig_01_real_neuron_gallery.svg`

Gallery of four neuron morphologies: pyramidal, Purkinje-like, motor, and sensory-like. The cells have different branching patterns, but each can still be analyzed by asking where input, trigger, and output occur.

## `fig_02_neuron_map.svg`

Simplified neuron cartoon with dendrites, soma, axon hillock, axon, terminals, and an arrow for action-potential direction. The image is a conceptual map, not a claim that every neuron has this exact shape.

## `fig_03_ion_gradients_barplot.svg`

Grouped bar chart comparing approximate inside and outside concentrations of sodium, potassium, chloride, and calcium. Potassium is high inside, while sodium and calcium are high outside.

## `fig_04_electrochemical_push.svg`

Membrane diagram showing potassium concentration push outward and electrical pull inward. The figure emphasizes that chemical and electrical forces can oppose each other.

## `fig_05_membrane_potential_measurement.svg`

A cell membrane is shown with one electrode inside, one reference electrode outside, and a voltmeter reading -70 mV. Small charge symbols near the membrane indicate that membrane potential is a voltage difference.

## `fig_06_synapse_and_psps.svg`

A presynaptic terminal releases neurotransmitter across a synaptic cleft onto postsynaptic receptors. Two inset traces show an EPSP moving toward threshold and an IPSP moving away from threshold.

## `fig_07_summation_graph.svg`

Voltage-time graph with a baseline near -70 mV and a threshold line near -55 mV. Several EPSP and IPSP effects combine into a single membrane-potential trace.

## `fig_08_action_potential_phases.svg`

Annotated action-potential trace showing rest, threshold, overshoot, repolarization, after-hyperpolarization, and refractory recovery. The axes are time in milliseconds and membrane potential in millivolts.

## `anim_01_action_potential_storyboard.svg`

Eight storyboard panels pair action-potential phases with channel states. Each frame includes a small voltage-trace inset and a channel cartoon.

## `fig_09_spike_raster_and_rate.svg`

Raster plot comparing weak, medium, and strong touch conditions. Spike ticks have equal height, but stronger conditions show more frequent spikes and higher rate labels.

## `fig_10_HH_circuit.svg`

Equivalent circuit diagram with membrane capacitance, sodium, potassium, and leak conductance branches. The diagram labels V_m, C_m, g_Na, g_K, reversal potentials, and applied current.

## `fig_11_patch_clamp_trace.svg`

A patch pipette records from a small membrane patch containing a single ion channel. A paired current trace jumps between closed and open levels over time.

## `channel_state_diagram.svg`

Three ion-channel cartoons show closed, open, and inactivated states. Arrows indicate possible transitions, and only the open state allows ions to pass.

## `fig_12_noise_to_jitter_panel.svg`

Multi-panel figure comparing repeated voltage traces, spike rasters, and a CV summary for low- and high-noise conditions. The high-noise condition shows more spread in spike timing.

## `isi_histogram_cv_panel.svg`

Panel with spike-time ticks, an interspike-interval histogram, and a summary box. The box shows mean ISI, standard deviation of ISI, and CV calculation.
