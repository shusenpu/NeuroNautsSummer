## Vocabulary

## Graph Reading

## Short Explanation

## Quantitative

7. A channel is open for 18 ms during a 90 ms recording. What is the open fraction?
8. A spike train has mean ISI = 40 ms and ISI standard deviation = 12 ms. What is the CV?

## Challenge Extension
